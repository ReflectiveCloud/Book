# Support and Feedback

## Support

Feel free to reach out to us with any questions or for help. 

As a first port of call, we encourage questions in our slack channel for visibility among the whole community. We add new users to this slack channel when they join the Hub, but remind us via [this form](https://tally.so/r/A7LyD0) to join the slack channel.

If you want to reach us directly, email alistair [at] reflective.org or ask questions via the feedback form below.
